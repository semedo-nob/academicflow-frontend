# AcademicFlow User Guide (Institutions)

Institution administrators and chairs use AcademicFlow to manage academic operations:

- Organization tree (schools / departments)
- Lecturers and academic units
- Teaching requests and recommendations
- Allocation, workload, timetable
- Conflicts and approvals
- Import / export and reports

Sign in at `/login` after your institution is approved by the AcademicFlow product owner.

You cannot access `/platform` — that console is for the product owner only.
